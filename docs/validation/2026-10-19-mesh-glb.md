are retained verbatim and **uninspected**, so paths/metadata may survive; inventory
makes no sanitization claim.

bilinear validity adds **zero** newly supported interiors and loses approximately
**14.47–14.65%** of native-known interiors. Maximum unquantized filter RGB difference
is **0.09915642**; maximum combined color difference is **0.09907440**. These are
sampled partial-mesh comparisons, not room coverage or continuous error bounds.

full **31,456/60,930 vertices** and **46,323/80,912 triangles** remain resident.

illumination remains baked; scale, gravity, collision and walking are unmeasured.
